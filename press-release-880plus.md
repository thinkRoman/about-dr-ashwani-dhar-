## TrPharma Launches 880 PLUS: A Physician-Formulated Daily Multivitamin Bridging the Modern Nutrition Gap

*The 13-vitamin, 8-mineral daily foundation arrives with a "balanced nutrition, not megadoses" philosophy and global-certified manufacturing*

**NEW DELHI / HYDERABAD, India** — TrPharma, the pharmaceutical division of ThinkRoman Ventures, today announced the launch of **880 PLUS**, a complete daily multivitamin containing 13 essential vitamins and 8 vital minerals, formulated to help close the micronutrient gaps that modern diets and lifestyles routinely leave behind.

880 PLUS is defined by a disciplined formulation philosophy: adequate, evidence-aligned doses of vitamins A, C, E, the full B-complex, and essential minerals — delivering everyday support for energy metabolism, immune function, cognitive clarity, and skin health without resorting to megadose marketing. Every ingredient in the formula is dosed in line with established dietary guidelines and traceable to independent nutrient reference data (the National Institutes of Health Office of Dietary Supplements).

**Physician-led from first principles.** The product is formulated under the direct guidance of **Dr. Ashwani Dhar, MD, DMI** — a physician with more than a decade of clinical care who founded ThinkRoman Ventures and serves as CEO of TrPharma.

> "As a physician, I've seen how quietly nutritional deficiency erodes energy, immunity, and quality of life. 880 PLUS is our answer: balanced, science-backed nutrition that works *with* the body — not against it. It's not about megadoses; it's about getting the foundation right, every single day." — **Dr. Ashwani Dhar, MD, DMI, CEO, TrPharma**

**Manufactured to global standards.** 880 PLUS is produced in facilities certified to **WHO-GMP, USFDA, ISO 22000:2018, ISO 9001:2015, and FSSAI** standards, with rigorous quality control at every stage of production.

**Key product highlights:**
- 13 essential vitamins + 8 vital minerals in one daily tablet
- NIH-referenced, evidence-aligned dosing
- B-complex support for energy metabolism and cognitive function
- Vitamins A, C, and E for immune and antioxidant support
- Folic acid and minerals supporting DNA synthesis and cellular health
- Worldwide-certified manufacturing (WHO-GMP, USFDA, FSSAI, ISO)

880 PLUS joins the TrPharma family of targeted formulations — **280 PLUS** for prenatal nutrition and **Sohar-D 60K** once-weekly Vitamin D — under a brand promise of quality first, science-backed formulation, and daily convenience.

**Availability.** 880 PLUS is now available for purchase through TrPharma's trusted retail partners, including the **Pharma247** platform and leading Indian online-pharmacy network **1mg**.
